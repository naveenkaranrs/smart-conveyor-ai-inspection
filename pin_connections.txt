AI-ENABLED QUALITY INSPECTION SYSTEM - DETAILED WIRING GUIDE (FINAL)
=============================================================================

1. POWER SUPPLY SETUP
--------------------------------------------------
Component | Terminal | Connect To
----------|----------|------------------------------------
L298N     | 12V      | +9V Battery / Adapter (Positive)
L298N     | GND      | Battery Negative (-) AND STM32 GND
L298N     | 5V       | STM32 5V, ESP32 Vin, IR VCC, Ultrasonic VCC


2. STM32 F103RB TO L298N (MOTOR CONTROL)
--------------------------------------------------
L298N Pin | STM32 Pin | Function
----------|-----------|----------------------------------
ENA       | PA0       | Speed Control (PWM) - Motor Pair 1
IN1       | PA1       | Motor A Direction 1
IN2       | PA4       | Motor A Direction 2
IN3       | PB0       | Motor B Direction 1
IN4       | PB1       | Motor B Direction 2
ENB       | PB10      | Speed Control (PWM) - Motor Pair 2


3. L298N TO MOTORS
--------------------------------------------------
OUT1 & OUT2 -> Motor Pair 1 (first two motors)
OUT3 & OUT4 -> Motor Pair 2 (next two motors)


4. SENSOR CONNECTIONS
--------------------------------------------------
SENSOR: **IR SENSOR** (Object Detection)
  - VCC   -> L298N 5V output
  - GND   -> Common GND
  - OUT   -> STM32 PB12

SENSOR: **ULTRASONIC SENSOR (HC-SR04)**
  - VCC   -> L298N 5V output
  - GND   -> Common GND
  - TRIG  -> STM32 PB13
  - ECHO  -> STM32 PB14


5. COMMUNICATION: STM32 <-> ESP32 (UART1)
--------------------------------------------------
*** IMPORTANT: DO NOT USE PA2/PA3 ***
*** PA2/PA3 are used by USB Serial Monitor ***

STM32 Pin  | ESP32 Pin    | Function
-----------|--------------|----------------------------------
PA9  (TX1) | GPIO 16 (RX) | STM32 sends data to ESP32
PA10 (RX1) | GPIO 17 (TX) | ESP32 sends commands to STM32
GND        | GND          | COMMON GROUND (MANDATORY!)


6. POWER RAILS SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------
VCC (+5V):  L298N 5V -> STM32 5V -> ESP32 Vin -> IR VCC -> Ultrasonic VCC
GND (0V):   Battery(-) -> L298N GND -> STM32 GND -> ESP32 GND -> IR GND -> Ultrasonic GND


IMPORTANT REMINDERS:
- L298N "5V Enable" jumper must be ON
- All GNDs MUST be connected together
- ESP32 only supports 2.4GHz WiFi
